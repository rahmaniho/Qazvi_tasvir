About Qazvin Tasvir Company

More than 31 years of trust, from Qazvin to global markets

Qazvin Tasvir Company (limited liability) began official operations in May 1995 under the leadership of Mr Saeed Aminzadeh. Over more than three decades of continuous service to the country's manufacturing and commercial sectors, we have become one of the most reputable and reliable names in two vital fields: trading, import and export, and transport and factory staff transport.

  • Uncompromising commitment to precise scheduling and full discipline in every project
  • A broad network of trusted domestic and international suppliers and customs brokers
  • Round-the-clock 24/7 support and continuous on-site supervision of the transport fleet
