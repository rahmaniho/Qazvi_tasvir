Frequently asked questions

Questions from factory managers and welfare departments

If your question is not answered here, our specialists are ready to respond by phone or WhatsApp

What is the minimum number of portions for a contract?

Usually from 50 portions upwards; for smaller organisations, a contract or combined portions are still possible depending on the delivery distance and the number of meals. After the site visit, our specialist provides the most accurate estimate.

Can the menu be changed?

Yes. The menu is weekly and seasonal and can be revised to suit your staff's preferences, your budget and your production schedule. Unpopular items are removed or replaced, and you can add diet, vegetarian or low-salt options.

Is corporate catering part of wages?

Under Iranian labour law, food provided to staff counts as a non-cash benefit and carries its own insurance and tax implications. The contract sets out how meals are calculated and documented, and we recommend consulting your own financial and insurance adviser before signing.

How is the meal delivery schedule set?

According to your working shifts; the delivery plan is defined in the contract and can also be arranged for night shifts, holidays and peak production days. Temporary changes are applied with prior coordination and in the shortest possible time.
